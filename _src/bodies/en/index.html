FAQ

Questions

What people ask us most about arranko.

What is arranko?

A nutrition app with artificial intelligence: take a photo, say or type what you ate, and see your calories and macros straight away (protein, carbs and fat). It is for anyone who wants to know what they eat, and especially useful if you train.

How does it know the calories from a photo?

You take a photo of your plate and the AI recognises the foods and estimates the amounts. arranko works out the calories and macros with its food database. You can also scan a barcode, or say or type what you ate.

Are the estimates exact?

They are estimates, and the app says so. Tap any food to change the amount and everything updates. With a barcode you use the real values from the label.

How are my targets worked out?

From your age, sex, height, weight, activity and goal. It never recommends fewer calories than your body burns at rest.

Is it free?

Yes, you can use it for free. The Free Plan includes 10 AI logs a month and 5 favourites, and logging by hand or by barcode has no limit. Premium gives you unlimited AI logs (within fair use), with a 7-day free trial.

Who is it for?

For anyone aged 16 and over who wants to know what they eat. If you train (running, cycling, padel, the gym or whatever moves you), it helps you give your body what it needs. It is not a diet app: food is fuel.

Is my data safe?

It is stored in the European Union, your meal photos are private, there are no ads and no selling of data, and you can delete your account and everything in it whenever you like. More in the Privacy Policy.

What language is the app in?

English for now. Spanish and Portuguese are coming soon.
