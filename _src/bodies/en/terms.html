Who we are

arranko is an app to log what you eat and know your calories and macros. With artificial intelligence it estimates your meals from a photo or from what you say or type, and you can also scan a product's barcode or add foods by hand. It helps you follow your calorie, protein, carbs and fat targets, your streak and your weight.

arranko is run by Zaymelith Bonalde, an individual based in Portugal. You can write to us at support@arranko.app.

Who can use arranko

You must be 16 or older. You need one account, with an email address you can open, and you keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens in your account.

Not medical advice

arranko gives estimates and general targets, not medical or nutrition advice. AI estimates can be wrong: check them and adjust them. Your targets come from standard formulas and the details you give, and the app never suggests a calorie target below what your body burns at rest.

Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before big changes to how you eat, if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition, or have had a difficult relationship with food. If you feel that tracking food is hurting you, stop and ask for help.
