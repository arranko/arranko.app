Privacy Policy

arranko helps you log what you eat and see your calories and macros. To do that it keeps some personal data, including data about your body. Here is what we keep, why, who helps us, and how you stay in control.

The short version

  • We keep only what the app needs to work: your account, your profile, your goals and what you log.
  • A photo, a typed description or a voice note goes to Google's Gemini AI only when you ask for an estimate, and only after you said yes in the app.
  • Your meal photos are private: only you can see them.
  • Your data is stored in the EU. We never sell it, there are no ads, and we don't use it to train AI.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, from the app.

AI estimates

When you ask arranko to estimate a meal from a photo, a typed description or a voice note, we send it through our server to Google Gemini, Google's AI, which returns the foods and amounts it sees. This happens only when you ask for an estimate, and only after you said yes to the question the app shows the first time. You can turn it off again in Settings.

We use Google's paid service, under which Google does not use what we send to improve its products. Google may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse. We don't keep your voice notes; a photo is kept only if you save the meal with it. We don't use your data to train any AI.

An estimate is a suggestion that you can always change, and your targets come from standard formulas with the details you give. arranko makes no decision about you that has legal or similarly significant effects.

Barcodes and reminders

When you scan a barcode, the app looks the product up in Open Food Facts, a free public food database. Only the barcode number is sent, nothing about you.

Meal and weigh-in reminders are set up on your phone itself; we don't send push notifications from a server and don't collect a notification id.

Crash reports and anonymous usage

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report so we can fix it: the error, the app version and the phone model, never your meals, photos or body data. We use Sentry for this, on its servers in the EU.

The app also counts how it is used, for example which screens people open and whether they come back, so we can improve it. These counts use a random id that isn't your name or email, and are kept by PostHog on its servers in the EU. You can stop them at any time with Share anonymous usage in Settings.

We never sell your data, never share it with advertisers and never use it to show ads. We share it with the police or a court only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything you logged: until you delete your account. Deleting removes it at once; our backups are overwritten within 7 days.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Anonymous usage counts: up to 12 months.
  • Account emails: Resend keeps a record of each email sent for up to 30 days.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to help you, and at most 2 years.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • See the data we keep about you and get a copy in a common format you can take elsewhere.
  • Correct it: most of it you can change yourself in the app.
  • Delete it: Settings → Delete Account, or see Delete your account.
  • Take back your consent to AI estimates (Settings) or to anonymous usage (Share anonymous usage in Settings).
  • Object to or limit how we use your data.

Write to privacy@arranko.app from the email address of your account. We answer within one month. If you think we got something wrong, you can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD, or the authority in your own country.

Security

Your data travels encrypted (HTTPS), and the database only lets each account read its own data. Meal photos are private and shown through links that expire after an hour.

If a data breach ever puts your data at risk, we tell the CNPD within 72 hours and tell you without delay when the risk to you is high, as the GDPR requires.

Age

arranko is for people aged 16 and over. If you think someone younger has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

This website

arranko.app uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and its fonts load from arranko.app itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change matters, for example a new service that handles your data, we tell you in the app before it applies.
